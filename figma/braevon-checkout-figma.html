How to read this file

Four phone frames of the Braevon checkout (screen 34), rebuilt as plain flex markup so an importer maps every container to a Figma auto-layout frame. Frames are 390 px wide; everything inside is fluid (100% width or flex:1), so stretching a frame reflows the layout instead of scaling it.

Import: html.to.design → paste the published URL of this page. Each .frame becomes one Figma frame, named by its id.

Structure rules used: no absolute positioning, no wrapper that holds a single child, one flex direction per container, spacing by gap only (never margins between siblings), images as real files in assets/.

Type

  • Plus Jakarta Sans throughout (400/500/600/700/800).
  • H1 26 px / 800 / centred · Section H2 22 px / 800 / centred.
  • Body 15 px / 1.6 in muted; small print 13 px.
  • Pack name 22 px / 700 in accent; price 24 px / 800 underlined.

Spacing & shape

  • Page column: 16 px side padding, 32 px between blocks.
  • Cards: 20 px padding, 16 px radius, shadow 0 6 24 rgba(16,20,34,.08).
  • Buttons and inputs: 10 px radius; pack cards 8 px.
  • Rows inside a card: 16 px gap; list items 10–12 px.

States & behaviour

  • Pack selection: 6 Pack is selected on load. Selected = 1 px #F3A184 border plus 3 px rgba(230,67,13,.15) glow. Selecting a pack re-renders the pack tag, the render, the SAVE disc (12 Pack only) and every price on the page.
  • Countdown: starts at 15:00 and ticks down once per second in three places (top bar, reserved pill, ready block). Same value in all three.
  • Offer popup: fires 10 s after load, once per visit, never again once claimed. Claiming applies −25 % to every price and keeps the reader in place — no scroll, no navigation. Dismiss with the X, the scrim or Esc.
  • Discounted price: original struck through in grey, new price beside it in green.
  • Authorization note: under Continue; first sentence always visible, the rest behind Read more / Read less (frame 04 shows it open).
  • Name: the first name from the intake form is echoed in the top bar and the H1, trimmed before the apostrophe (“Sabbir’s”).

Assets

  • assets/pack-6.png, assets/pack-12.png — product renders (transparent PNG).
  • assets/tablets.png, assets/onset-chart.svg (static export of the animated chart).
  • assets/braevon-wordmark.svg, assets/logos/*.svg.
  • Stars, ticks and glyphs are text characters here; swap for the production icon set.
